About the firm

Pierre Policy & Strategy Consulting LLC is a minority-owned small business that provides conflict analysis, policy research, and data analysis to federal agencies, prime contractors, and nonprofit partners. The firm is registered in SAM.gov and eligible for federal awards.

Our approach is simple: start from the data, read the local sources, and deliver analysis a decision-maker can use the same day. We pair indicator work and dashboards with clear written interpretation, and our French, Haitian Creole, and Spanish capability gives us direct access to reporting that English-only teams miss. Haiti and the wider Caribbean are a particular focus.

PP&SC is led by founder Dakendjy Pierre, whose background spans peacebuilding research, governance and human-rights analysis, and graduate study in international affairs.
