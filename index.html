Conflict analysis and policy research for decision-makers.

Core capabilities

01

Conflict & Crisis Analysis

Conflict monitoring, early-warning indicators, and displacement tracking built on open-source datasets such as ACLED and IOM DTM.

02

Policy Research & Analytical Writing

Policy briefs, issue memos, literature reviews, and background papers on security, defense and alliance policy, and international institutions, written for senior audiences.

03

Data Analysis & Visualization

Indicator analysis, data-quality review, and interactive dashboards that pair the numbers with written interpretation.

04

Governance, Human Rights & Peacebuilding

Indicator frameworks, social-cohesion and civic-inclusion analysis, and research support for program design.

05

Multilingual Research

Source research and translation in French, Haitian Creole, and Spanish, giving direct access to local reporting that English-only teams miss.
